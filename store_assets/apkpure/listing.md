## 长描述（English）

Hantang TCM is an **offline-first, completely free** study and reference companion for classical Chinese herbal medicine, faithfully recreating Master Ni Haixia's Renji teaching system.

No sign-up. Core content runs entirely on your phone and works with no connection. There is also an optional "Ask Master Ni AI" feature: it stays off until you enter your own API endpoint and key, and produces no network traffic while off.

**Classical texts**
- Shang Han Lun: 394 chapters (390 Song-edition clauses + 4 appendices), ordered by Song-edition clause number
- Jin Gui Yao Lue: 433 chapters, broken down to clause level
- 82 chapters of the Ling Shu + 339 chapters of the Ming-dynasty Zhenjiu Dacheng
- Formula and herb names in the text jump to detail pages in one tap; "Found in the Classics" on a formula page marks its source clauses in reverse

**Formulas and materia medica**
- 322 classical formulas: composition, dosage, decoction and administration, contraindications, modification rules
- 465 materia medica entries with nature, flavour and meridian tropism, plus 335 lecture transcripts
- Fuzi is split into raw and prepared entries per Master Ni's stance that the two cannot substitute for each other

**Acupuncture and pattern differentiation**
- 408 acupoints with location, needling, moxibustion, contraindications and clinical notes
- Fourteen-meridian circulation charts and a 93-point national-standard ear-acupoint library (GB/T 13734-2008)
- A Six-Meridian pattern-differentiation state machine with a dual-track differentiation tree: guided intake derives the meridian and the principal formula
- "Patient colloquial profiles": classical clauses rendered back into plain bedside language, checkable line by line
- 1257 case records from Master Ni, bidirectionally linked with formulas

**Reading experience**
- One-tap Simplified/Traditional Chinese toggle (OpenCC dictionary-level); Traditional Chinese input is searchable too
- Font size up to 200%, dark mode
- Global search spanning lecture transcripts and original classic texts; 1728 indexed entries

**Also included**
- Traditional-culture reference modules: Five Movements and Six Qi, Zi Wei Dou Shu, Yi Jing, Four Pillars, Six-Yao
- Fully open source (Apache 2.0). No ads, no in-app purchases, no tracking, no personal data uploaded

**Disclaimer**
This app is a study and reference tool for classical Chinese formulas and traditional culture. It does not provide medical services, and no pattern-differentiation result or formula suggestion can replace diagnosis and treatment by a licensed physician. Follow your doctor's advice when taking medication; seek immediate medical care for acute or severe conditions. The Zi Wei Dou Shu, Yi Jing and Four Pillars modules are traditional folk-culture reference content, unrelated to medicine, and do not constitute any prediction or advice.
